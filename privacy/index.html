privacy

what the reading room saves, why, and how to get it deleted. short version: your private words never leave your phone, i don't sell anything about you, and you can ask me to delete it all.

the reading room is run by Unfiltered Projects LLC, 9461 Charleville Blvd #1020, Beverly Hills, CA 90212.
questions or requests: team@unfilteredprojects.com

what stays on your phone

your notes, unsent texts, letters and spiral entries are kept in your browser on your phone. they never reach me. nobody reads them, including me.

what i save

  • what you give me: your name, email and country.
  • your results: which room you started, your clock, your quiz type or pattern, and simple counts (like how many urges you logged). never the words themselves.
  • how you use the site: which pages and sections you open, what you tap, how long you stay, the link that brought you here, your device type, time zone and language.
  • my emails: when an email was sent, whether it was opened (some emails carry a tiny invisible image that tells me when it loads) and which links you tap.
  • if you buy: Stan handles the payment. i get your name, email and what you bought. i never see your card.

why

to save your progress so it follows you, to send you my emails, and to see which parts actually help so i can make them better.

emails

when you give me your email for a room, you'll get a short series of emails from me, my free tuesday letter invite and the occasional offer. every email has an unsubscribe link that works in one tap. you can also reply and ask.

who helps me run it

  • Google stores the data and sends the emails.
  • GitHub hosts this site.
  • Stan takes payments, and Zapier passes purchases to my records.
  • Substack runs the tuesday letter, under its own privacy policy, if you subscribe there.
  • the fonts load from Google Fonts.

i don't sell your data, i don't share it for ads and there are no ad trackers on this site.

how long i keep it

as long as you're on my list or using a room. ask me and i'll delete everything about you within 30 days. clearing your browser deletes what's on your phone.

your rights

wherever you live, you can ask what i hold about you, ask me to fix it, or ask me to delete it. email team@unfilteredprojects.com.

UK and EU: i use your email because you gave it to me for a room, and you can take that back any time by unsubscribing or emailing me. you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California: you have the right to know, delete and correct your information, and i don't sell or share it.

the small print

the reading room is for adults 18 and over. it's not therapy or medical advice. if you're in danger, call or text 988 in the US, or find a free line in your country at findahelpline.com.
